# Four supports make no pressure

Two tanks and four supports is too much sustain and not enough burst to stop anyone pushing. Supports deal damage but cannot focus it or sustain it the way a damage kit does, and without the threat of damage picks the enemy holds no fear of stepping into the open, so the tanks get no space to take. It charges a flat point and a half once four supports are on the six.
