# Reach the flier

When red fields a pick that flies, the six needs a gun whose published range reaches the air, because a flier picks the distance of every exchange. Cassidy and Ashe apply excellent pressure on Mercy, Pharah and Echo through damage projection at range, and a Pharah blows up a Torbjörn's turrets from a distance he cannot answer. The longest published range on the six is measured, read while red fields at least one flier.
