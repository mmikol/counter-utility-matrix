# Brawl splashes the scrum

A brawl six fights where both teams stand within arm's reach, and area damage hits everything in that press without aiming at any of it. Junkrat (5 pieces), Venture (4) and D.Mon, Lúcio, Baptiste and Wuyang (3 each) carry the most area damage among brawl picks; Reinhardt carries none tagged. Measured as kit pieces tagged area of effect, read only while brawl is the majority style.
