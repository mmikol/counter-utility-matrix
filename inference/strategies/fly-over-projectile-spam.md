# Fly over a projectile team

A red team whose guns are mostly projectiles cannot lead a target in the air, and a flier duels projectile-only heroes at a large advantage. Pharah is named as one of the best picks into Torbjörn because he is not hitscan, and a Junkrat and Mei pair into an air pick is told it will be farmed. Measured as the count of picks that fly or hover, read while 4 or more red picks carry projectile weapons.
