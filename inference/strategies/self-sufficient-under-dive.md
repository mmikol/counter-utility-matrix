# Self-sufficient under a dive

A dive lands on the supports first, and the picks that heal themselves are the ones still fighting while the supports are busy living. The low-rank situations guide's answer to a team that cannot be healed is picks that are more self-sufficient, and a D.Mon player explains adapting to a low-healing backline by playing health packs and angles. Measured as the count of picks carrying any healing at all, read while 4 or more red picks carry a movement tool.
