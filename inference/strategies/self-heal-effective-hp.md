# Self-heal is effective HP

Every kit that heals adds effective hit points that no enemy sees on the bar. Baptiste's regen burst makes him 350 effective HP in a duel, and a Moira's fade and drain add effective HP on top of her pool, so two sixes with the same summed pool are not equally hard to kill. Summed peak single heal across all picks, any role, is measured.
