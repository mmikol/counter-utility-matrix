# Boosted guns two-tap squishies

A red with two damage amplifiers, a Mercy beam, a Discord, a Nano Boost or Baptiste's window, turns a 170 damage shot into a kill on any 225 pick in two hits, and nothing heals a headshot. Against that line the comp wants as few picks at 250 or under as the shape allows. Measured as our picks at or under 250 pool, kept low while 2 or more red picks amplify damage.
