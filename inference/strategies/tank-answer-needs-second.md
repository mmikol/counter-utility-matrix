# A tank answer needs a second

When red fields two tanks, a single answer to either is a duel the answering pick may lose, and the six should hold two answers for its tank matchups. Zarya into Mauga farms free charge but needs support help to not get overrun once she is out of bubbles, and a tank with no hard counter needs teamwork with the damage heroes to shut down. Measured as the count of revealed red picks answered by two or more of ours, while red fields two tanks.
