# Seat one real healer

A support line heals at the roster's bench or the six is not sustained at all. One main healer with high burst healing is the pairing the game rewards, and a Lúcio, Brigitte or Mizuki alone cannot be relied on as the main healer because the single-target heal is never enough. Measured as the supports' summed heal peak over the roster's two-support bench.
