# Two answers to the must-ban

A hero the lobby usually bans is a hero one answer does not solve, so when one stands on red the six wants two picks that answer it. Mauga, the ban of choice for many tank players, has two answers on almost every map, and one answer that a ban or a swap removes leaves nothing. Measured as the count of revealed red picks answered by two or more of ours, while red's highest ban rate is 30 percent or more.
