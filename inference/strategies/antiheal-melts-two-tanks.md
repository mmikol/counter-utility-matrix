# Anti-heal melts a tank pair

Two tanks on red are 900 to 1200 hit points that only die with their healing switched off, and anti-heal is the tool the community names first for every heavy tank from Mauga to Reinhardt. A grenade on a tank at half health turns a sustained trade into a kill, which is why Ana's grenade is listed beside Defense Matrix as the strongest ability against Mauga. Measured as the count of picks with anti-heal, read while red fields 2 tanks.
