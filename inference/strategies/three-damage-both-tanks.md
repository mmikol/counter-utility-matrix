# Three damage need both tanks

Three damage picks are carried by two tanks or not at all. A solo tank in front of a damage-heavy six has to survive, protect the backline and take ground without an off-tank's cover, and a damage line with no front to shoot from is three flankers hoping for picks. It charges a flat point and a half once three or more damage picks sit in front of fewer than two tanks.
