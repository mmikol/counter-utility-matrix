# Saves against loaded ultimates

When red carries four or more damage ultimates, the biggest single save on our side is what turns a wipe into a fight. Sound Barrier protecting from D.Va's bomb and burst damage in general is what kept GOATS alive, and a Biotic Grenade that brings four teammates back from critical is the play that beats a combo. Measured as our biggest single heal minus red's biggest hit, read while red fields four or more ultimates that carry a damage figure.
