# Boost a hitscan pair

Damage amplification is worth most on picks that land every shot, so a six with two hitscan guns has the right targets for a Mercy beam, a Nano or a Discord. A pocketed hitscan takes over games because a 170-damage boosted shot or a 190 dynamite crosses the one-shot line on a 225 pool, and the pocket is what puts it there. Measured as the count of picks that amplify someone's damage, read while the six carries 2 or more hitscan picks.
