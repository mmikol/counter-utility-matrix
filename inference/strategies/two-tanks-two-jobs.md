# A main tank and an off-tank

A tank pair wants a main tank that sets the fight and an off-tank that covers the angle, not two of the same. The main tank establishes how and when the fight happens while the off-tank manages the high ground and peels, and a pair of two off-tanks has nobody to walk behind. Distinct subroles divided by picks across the six is read, only while two tanks are on the six, so two tanks of one job lower it.
