# A barrier blocks the big hit

When red carries a single hit of 500 or more, a Self-Destruct, a RIP-Tire or an Orisa spear, no heal arrives in time and the only answer is something that takes the hit instead of a player. The best counter to a Reinhardt ultimate is a Reinhardt barrier, and shields to block a D.Va bomb are what low ranks ask their tank for. Measured as our picks with a barrier, read while red's biggest single hit is 500 or more.
